# UI/UX & Design Guidelines for ats book

## Theme & Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** Deep Slate / Soft Indigo (Professional & relaxing for eyes)
- **Background (Light Mode):** Soft Off-White `#F9F9FB` (Prevents eye strain)
- **Background (Dark Mode):** Charcoal Dark `#121212`
- **Text Color:** Charcoal `#1E1E1E` (Light Mode) / Soft Grey `#E0E0E0` (Dark Mode)

## Typography & Reading Experience
- **Story Body Font:** Readable Serif or Soft Sans-Serif font.
- **Line Height:** 1.5x for effortless long reading.
- **Controls:** Floating bar or top bar to quickly change font size (Small, Medium, Large) and toggle Dark/Light mode.

## Navigation Structure
1. **Home Screen:** Search bar, Categories, Featured Story Banner, Story List.
2. **Story Details Screen:** Clean distraction-free reading interface with progress bar.
3. **Bookmarks Screen:** Saved stories list.
